# Data Release Policy

- Synthetic examples only.
- No customer, lead, owner, property, phone, email, financial, behavioral,
  authentication, or operator data.
- No production receipts or snapshots.
- No derived weights or features calibrated from private datasets.
- No customer-specific connector payloads.
- No real infrastructure identifiers.
- Public datasets require an independent rights, provenance, privacy, and
  re-identification review.
